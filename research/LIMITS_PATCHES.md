## Proposed bullets (edit for house style)

1. **McNamara / problem selection:** AI tools can accelerate measurable topics while shrinking collective focus (Hao et al., *Nature* 2026; Bisht et al. arXiv:2605.08956). Explicit `ValueProfile` is required so ranking does not pretend to be value-free.

2. **Not EVSI:** Axis scores are proxies inspired by VOI/ITN, not ISPOR EVPI/EVSI/ENBS estimates. See `research/VOI_APPROXIMATIONS.md`.

3. **Not emotion recognition:** Epistemic cues and emotion mixes are UX annotations (`annotation_only`), not biometric ERS under the EU AI Act. See `research/AFFECTIVE_SAFETY.md`.

4. **Hivemind:** Multi-model generation can still homogenize open-ended ideation (Jiang et al. Artificial Hivemind; Bisht hypothesis-hivemind). Diversity stages and disagreement metrics matter more than model count.

5. **Ideation ≠ execution:** LLM ideas that look strong pre-execution can weaken after real work (Si et al. ideation–execution gap). Ranked unknowns are investigation candidates, not guaranteed programs.

6. **OpenAlex metadata:** Hit counts and optional funder fields are incomplete/uneven; never treat as calibrated neglectedness. See `research/FUNDING_NEGLECT_SIGNALS.md`.

7. **Dual-use filters:** Heuristic risk scores are not a biosecurity authority. See `research/DUAL_USE_RANKING.md`.
