## 1. What it is

Intern-Atlas builds a **methodological evolution graph** from >1M AI papers:

- Method-level entities  
- Lineage / adaptation edges grounded in verbatim evidence  
- Bottlenecks driving transitions  
- Temporal tree search for evolution chains  
- Downstream demos: idea evaluation + generation  

Positioned as research infrastructure for AI scientists (document-centric cites → method topology).
